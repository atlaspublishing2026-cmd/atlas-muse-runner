# Car Audio Estimate Calculator

A car audio estimate should include more than the visible equipment. The hidden cost is usually labor: disassembly, OEM integration, wiring, fabrication, tuning, testing, and final quality assurance.

## What a useful estimate calculator should capture

- Shop labor rate
- Estimated installation hours
- Vehicle teardown and reassembly
- OEM integration
- Wiring and power distribution
- Amplifier and DSP setup
- Subwoofer enclosure or fabrication work
- Sound deadening
- Tuning
- Final testing and QA
- Accessories and miscellaneous shop supplies

## Why quick quotes go wrong

A quote that only prices the radio, speakers, amplifier, or subwoofer can hide the time needed to integrate the system into the vehicle. That creates margin leakage and makes otherwise profitable jobs look unprofitable after the work is finished.
