# 12-Volt Shop Quote Checklist

Use this checklist before sending a quote for car audio, remote start, security, lighting, cameras, tint-related electronics, or other mobile-electronics work.

## Vehicle

- Year, make, model
- Trim level
- Factory audio system
- Factory amplifier
- Steering-wheel controls
- Factory camera / sensors
- Known integration requirements

## Labor

- Teardown and reassembly
- Wiring
- Equipment installation
- Fabrication
- Integration
- Programming
- Tuning
- Testing and QA

## Materials and add-ons

- Wire
- Fuse holders
- Distribution hardware
- Interfaces
- Mounting hardware
- Sound treatment
- Connectors
- Shop supplies

## Final checks

- All factory functions retained where required
- No warning lights
- No unwanted noise
- Proper gain structure
- Safe wiring and fuse protection
- Customer functions demonstrated
