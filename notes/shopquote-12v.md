# ShopQuote 12V — Car Audio & Mobile Electronics Estimate Calculator

ShopQuote 12V is a lightweight quoting calculator for car-audio, mobile-electronics, tint, security, remote-start, fabrication, lighting, and related 12-volt shops.

## What it solves

Fast estimates often hide real labor: OEM integration, teardown, fabrication, wiring, tuning, testing, final QA, and shop-specific add-ons. ShopQuote 12V makes those pieces visible before the quote leaves the shop.
